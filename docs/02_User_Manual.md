# User Manual - Exam Preparation Planner

## 1. Login / Register
- Open the app, enter your username & password, click LOGIN.
- If you don't have an account, click "New user? Create an account",
  fill the form and click REGISTER.

## 2. Dashboard
After logging in you land on the Dashboard. It shows:
- **Total Subjects** – how many subjects you have added
- **Upcoming Exams** – exams whose date is today or later
- **Completed Topics** – total topics marked completed across all subjects
- **Overall Progress** – completed topics ÷ total topics, as a percentage
- **Today's Study Plan** – topics planned for today's date
- **Upcoming Exams table** – nearest exams sorted by date

## 3. Subjects Menu
- Fill "Subject Name" and "Total Topics" and click **Add**.
- Click any row in the table to load it into the form, edit the values
  and click **Update**, or click **Delete** to remove it.
- Type in the Search box to filter subjects by name instantly.
- Duplicate subject names (for the same user) are not allowed.

## 4. Exams Menu
- Choose a Subject from the dropdown, enter Exam Name, Date (YYYY-MM-DD),
  Time and Venue, then click **Add**.
- Tick "Show Upcoming Exams Only" to hide past exams.
- Click any column heading to sort the table by that column.
- Select a row to Update or Delete it.

## 5. Study Planner Menu
- Choose Subject, type the Topic name, choose Plan Type
  (Daily / Weekly / Monthly), enter the date and study hours.
- Tick "Mark as Completed" if you have finished the topic.
- Click **Add**. Completed topics automatically update the Progress Tracker.
- Use the "View" dropdown to filter Daily/Weekly/Monthly plans.

## 6. Progress Tracker Menu
- Shows an overall progress bar at the top.
- Shows one progress bar per subject with completed/total/remaining topics.
- This updates automatically whenever you mark a topic completed in the Planner.

## 7. Reports Menu
- Choose a Report Type: Daily, Weekly, Monthly or Subject Report.
- For "Subject Report", also choose a Subject.
- Click **Generate Report** to view it in the box below.
- Click **Export to .txt** to save the report as a text file on your computer.

## 8. Logout
Click the "Logout" button at the bottom of the sidebar to return to the login screen.
